# book-mascots — project notes for Claude

## Known mascot-name collision: two Sylvias

There are currently two distinct mascots in this gallery that share the
first name **Sylvia**:

- **Sylvia the Statistical Squirrel** — mascot for the AP Statistics
  textbook (`docs/mascots/statistics-course/`).
- **Sylvia the Squirrel** — mascot for the Personal Finance with AI
  textbook (`docs/mascots/personal-finance/`).

This is acknowledged but not yet resolved. The collision is acceptable as
a soft visual link between the two quantitative-reasoning textbooks
(both squirrels, both about managing uncertainty over time), but it is a
known weakness for parasocial recall — a learner who has used both books
will not be able to refer to "Sylvia" unambiguously.

If a future revision wants to disambiguate, options include renaming the
personal-finance squirrel (e.g., to a different first name keeping the
saver-squirrel symbolism) or the statistics squirrel. Do not rename
without checking sibling textbook projects, since the mascot may be
referenced by name in chapter content of those books.

## Known mascot-name collision: three Sages

There are currently three distinct mascots in this gallery that share the
first name **Sage**:

- **Sage the Fox** — mascot for the Algebra I textbook
  (`docs/mascots/algebra-1/`).
- **Sage the Octopus** — mascot for the Modeling Healthcare Data with
  Graphs textbook (`docs/mascots/modeling-healthcare-data/`).
- **Sage the Owl** — mascot for the Systems Thinking in the Age of AI
  textbook (`docs/mascots/systems-thinking/`).

Unlike the two Sylvias above, these three subjects (algebra, healthcare
graph data, systems thinking) have no shared theme, so the collision isn't
even a soft visual link — it's just a coincidence of independently-chosen
"wise guide" names. This is acknowledged but not yet resolved, and it is a
sharper weakness for parasocial recall than the Sylvia case, since a
learner moving between any two of these three books has a 1-in-3 chance of
meeting a second "Sage" with a completely different species and personality.

If a future revision wants to disambiguate, rename two of the three and
keep the strongest name/species pairing as Sage (Sage the Owl has the
best claim, since the owl's panoramic field of view is a working metaphor
for "zooming out to see the whole system"). Do not rename without checking
sibling textbook projects, since the mascot may be referenced by name in
chapter content of those books.
