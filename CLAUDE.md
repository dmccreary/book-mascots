# book-mascots — project notes for Claude

## Known mascot-name collision: two Sylvias

There are currently two distinct mascots in this gallery that share the
first name **Sylvia**:

- **Sylvia the Statistical Squirrel** — mascot for the AP Statistics
  textbook (`docs/mascots/statistics-course/`).
- **Sylvia the Squirrel** — mascot for the Personal Finance with AI
  textbook (`docs/mascots/personal-finance/`).

This is acknowledged but not yet resolved. The collision is acceptable as
a soft visual link between the two quantitative-reasoning textbooks
(both squirrels, both about managing uncertainty over time), but it is a
known weakness for parasocial recall — a learner who has used both books
will not be able to refer to "Sylvia" unambiguously.

If a future revision wants to disambiguate, options include renaming the
personal-finance squirrel (e.g., to a different first name keeping the
saver-squirrel symbolism) or the statistics squirrel. Do not rename
without checking sibling textbook projects, since the mascot may be
referenced by name in chapter content of those books.
