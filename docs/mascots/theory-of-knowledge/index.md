# Theory Of Knowledge

**Sofia the Owl** is the mascot for the [Theory of Knowledge](https://dmccreary.github.io/theory-of-knowledge/) textbook. The name *Sofia* is the Greek root of *philosophy* (literally "love of wisdom") and the personification of wisdom in the early Christian tradition, while the owl is wisdom's most enduring visual symbol. Sofia was chosen because Theory of Knowledge — the IB Diploma's epistemology course — asks students to interrogate how we come to know what we know, and the mascot's name and species jointly invoke the long tradition of philosophical inquiry the course continues. The choice is strong because the name and species reinforce each other, and the owl's natural posture (still, watchful, asking *who?*) matches the disciplinary stance of the field.
