# Character Sheet: Armadillo

The canonical identity document for **Armadillo**, a general-purpose learning mascot for the mascot gallery. Every pose prompt and generated image should re-anchor to this description.

## Identity

- **Name:** Armadillo
- **Form / species:** Anthropomorphic armadillo
- **Role:** General-purpose resilience and careful-exploration mascot
- **Subject:** Useful across courses when learners need steadiness, curiosity, and protection from common mistakes
- **Audience:** General learners
- **Catchphrase:** "Check the path, then keep going."
- **Pronouns:** They/them

## Visual Description

- **Primary color:** Warm terracotta brown - hex `#A65F3A`
- **Accent color:** Deep teal - hex `#2A9D8F`
- **Other key colors:** Cream face and belly `#F4E6D0`, charcoal outlines `#3A3F44`, soft tan armor highlights `#D8A36A`
- **Form:** Small upright armadillo with a rounded segmented armor shell, compact body, short muzzle, small ears, curved tail, and stable feet
- **Face / expression:** Bright attentive eyes, gentle eyebrows, and a friendly closed-mouth smile
- **Arms / hands / expressive features:** Two simple arms with visible three-fingered hands; hands stay free for gestures
- **Clothing / accessories:** No clothing and no handheld objects; a small teal neckerchief is allowed as the only consistent accent
- **Signature identifiers:** Three clear armor bands across the back, cream muzzle and belly, terracotta shell, teal neckerchief, curved tail visible to one side
- **Size proportion:** Compact, icon-friendly character with a slightly large head, rounded shell silhouette, short limbs, and generous negative space around the full body
- **Art style:** Polished modern flat-vector illustration with clean dark outlines, simple geometric forms, minimal shading, and professional educational tone

## Personality

- Patient and steady under pressure
- Curious but cautious before acting
- Encouraging without being loud or flashy

## Voice

- Uses brief, plain-language guidance that helps learners slow down and notice structure
- Frames mistakes as signals to inspect rather than reasons to stop
- Encourages persistence, preparation, and careful checking
- Signature phrases: "Check the path, then keep going.", "Armor up your reasoning.", "Small steps still move forward."

## Why This Mascot

An armadillo is a strong general mascot because its shell naturally suggests protection, preparation, and resilience without implying aggression. The segmented armor gives the character a clear visual identity and a useful learning metaphor: break problems into protected, manageable parts. Armadillo works best as a calm companion for learners who need to pause, inspect, and continue.
