# Benchy Pedagogical Mascot Pose Guide

## Character Sheet

Benchy is a friendly anthropomorphic 3D-printing tugboat mascot inspired by the classic 3DBenchy calibration model. He has a bright blue printed-plastic body with subtle visible layer lines that resemble a real FDM 3D print. The lower hull is warm red-orange. His cabin contains a large expressive cartoon face with oversized blue-and-white eyes, thick expressive eyebrows, and a wide animated smile. Benchy has flexible cartoon-style white-gloved hands attached to soft bendable arms that emerge naturally from the sides of the hull. His smokestack remains intact and recognizable to preserve the silhouette of the original Benchy model.

Style: modern polished 3D cartoon render suitable for educational textbooks, websites, slides, stickers, classroom posters, and intelligent textbook interfaces.

Rendering requirements for ALL poses:

* PNG image format
* Fully transparent background
* No text in image
* Consistent lighting and proportions across all poses
* Character centered in frame
* High-resolution render
* Friendly educational tone
* Arms and hands highly expressive
* Preserve recognizable Benchy silhouette
* Slight visible 3D-print layer texture for authenticity
