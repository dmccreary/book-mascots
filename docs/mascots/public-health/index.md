# Introduction to Public Health

**Sage the Crane** is the mascot for [Introduction to Public Health](https://dmccreary.github.io/public-health/). Cranes move across wetlands and migration routes, offering a population-level perspective in which health depends on connected environments rather than isolated individuals. Sage denotes sound judgment, matching the evidence-based decisions required in prevention, surveillance, and health policy. Sage was chosen to reinforce the long view: notice patterns, protect communities, and act before preventable harm spreads. The advisory name and wide-ranging species provide complementary metaphors, making this a thoughtful and well-coupled public-health mascot.
