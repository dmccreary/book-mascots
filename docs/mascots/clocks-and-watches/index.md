# Clocks and Watches

**Chrono the Robot** is the mascot for [Clocks and Watches with AI](https://dmccreary.github.io/clocks-and-watches/), a textbook that teaches computational thinking by designing and building clocks and watches from low-cost parts with MicroPython on the Raspberry Pi Pico. Chrono is a small purple robot whose head is a round smartwatch display, complete with a dark bezel and a silver crown, and whose face is drawn on that screen in glowing teal lines — the same kind of round display the course builds toward, so every change of expression is literally a new image rendered on the hardware students wire up and program. The name comes from the Greek *khronos*, "time," the root of chronometer and chronograph, so it states the subject outright while staying short and gender-neutral. Chrono was chosen to install the idea that a clock face is not a fixed object but something a program draws, pixel by pixel, once a second, and the gear emblem on the chest keeps the older mechanical tradition of timekeeping in view alongside the digital one. That gives Chrono two load-bearing layers — a body built from the course's own display and a name that means the thing being measured — which places the mascot among the gallery's stronger pairings, with the caveat that a robot is the gallery's most common body plan for electronics books and so does less to set the character apart than the watch-face head does.
