# Food Science

**Zyme the Yeast Cell** is the mascot for [Food Science](https://dmccreary.github.io/food-science/). Yeast converts sugars through fermentation, producing carbon dioxide and flavor compounds central to bread, beverages, and many food-processing systems. Zyme comes from the Greek *zymē*, meaning leaven or ferment, the same root found in words such as enzyme. Zyme was chosen to make microscopic biological and chemical transformations visible as a recurring character. Because both the organism and the name encode a foundational food-science process, this is an exceptionally strong mascot choice.
