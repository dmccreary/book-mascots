# Sage the Fox Image Prompts

Generate seven consistent poses for one book mascot. Every prompt below is
self-contained and may be used independently.

## Neutral — `neutral.png`

Create Sage, a friendly gender-neutral pedagogical fox for an Algebra I textbook.
Sage has bright orange fur (`#F57C00`), a cream muzzle, chest, inner ears, and tail
tip, dark brown paws, large kind brown eyes, small round navy-blue glasses, and a
royal-blue scarf (`#1976D2`). Use a polished modern flat-vector cartoon style with
clean outlines and simple geometric forms. Show exactly one full-body character,
centered and uncropped. Sage stands upright facing the viewer in a relaxed neutral
pose, paws at their sides, with a calm closed-mouth smile. Use a perfectly flat
solid `#00FF00` chroma-key background with no shadows, gradients, texture, floor
plane, or green on the character. No text or watermark.
