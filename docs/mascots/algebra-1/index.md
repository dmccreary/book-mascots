# Algebra 1

**Sage the Fox** is the mascot for [Algebra I](https://dmccreary.github.io/algebra-1/), a textbook covering equation-solving, functions, and graphing. Foxes are a familiar cultural shorthand for quick, pattern-spotting intelligence — the trait a student needs most when hunting for the structure hidden inside a messy expression. The name Sage reinforces that framing without wordplay tied to algebra itself; it signals patient, seasoned guidance rather than a specific mathematical concept. Sage was chosen to model curiosity and persistence — pausing to look for the pattern rather than panicking at a wall of symbols, which is the disposition the course wants students to adopt toward multi-step problems. It is a good mascot — anthropomorphic, consistent, and thematically warm — but the fox-cleverness link is general rather than concept-specific, so it sits closer to the gallery's "good" tier than its "best" examples.
